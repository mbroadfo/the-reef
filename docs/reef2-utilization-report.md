# Reef 2.0: capital utilization, before and after the eligibility screen

Generated by `tools/utilization_diagnostic.py` from two real runs of the engine on the 70-session synthetic competition fixture (`tests/fixtures/competition_scenario.py`). Offline, deterministic, no market data, no network, no model. **It shows how the machinery behaves on invented prices; it is not evidence of investment skill, and the differences below are consequences to inspect, not targets.** No parameter was tuned to produce them.

- **Before**: `screen_untradable=False`. A name the gate would refuse (DEAL, a pending cash acquisition) can take one of a strategy's `max_candidates` places and D0's single new-position slot, and is then declined by the gate.
- **After** (the shipped default): such a name is withheld before ranking and recorded as `OPPORTUNITY_SUPPRESSED`. Same strategies, same ranking, same D0 slot, same Risk Engine limits, same C1 policy.
- Ledger heads: before `a565efd6ef55`, after `71d5e713b864`.

## 2. What bound each new position

The pod asks for a fraction of its sleeve cash; D0 sets a ceiling; the Risk Engine takes the smallest of risk-per-trade, position cap and cash. 'Request fraction' means the pod asked for less than every limit would have allowed.

**After**: 17 entries; binding: 7 position cap, 6 request fraction, 4 risk per trade. Asked $16,452, limits allowed $14,408, filled $12,691.

**Do untradable opportunities count toward C1's proposal-quality threshold?** No. C1 counts `PROPOSAL_SUBMITTED` events, the proposals Apex was shown, in the trailing window. A withheld signal is an `OPPORTUNITY_SUPPRESSED` event, never a proposal, so it cannot help a pod reach `min_proposals`. Before the correction a pending-deal name *was* submitted (and then declined by the gate), so it padded the count: Momentum's review-035 evidence cited 11 proposals, most of them DEAL. After it, the same review cites 2, below the unchanged threshold of 3, and the policy holds with `LEADER_CANNOT_DEPLOY` instead of moving $510. That is the policy working as written on cleaner evidence; no C1 value was altered.
