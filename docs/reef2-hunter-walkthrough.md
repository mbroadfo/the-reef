# Reef 2.0: the Hunter vertical slice, end to end

Generated by `tools/hunter_walkthrough.py` from a real run of the engine. **Everything below is a simulation on synthetic prices** (`tests/fixtures/hunter_scenario.py`), built so each behaviour happens on a known day. It shows that the machinery works and is reproducible. It is not evidence that the strategy makes money.

## Setup

- Fund: $10,000, all cash, epoch `epoch-1`. One sleeve, `hunter`, funded by D0 with 40% = $4,000; $6,000 stays unallocated.
- Strategy: `hunter-momentum` v1, spec hash `1711bcfc27aa6ff4`. Rules are in the docstring of `the_reef/strategy/hunter.py`: 20-session closing-high breakout, above the 50-session average, liquidity filter, stop at close minus 2 x ATR(14), trend-break exit below the 20-session average, stop only ever rises.
- Limits (unchanged): 10% per position, 0.5% of NAV at risk per position, 25% per sector (10% unknown), 60% invested, 2% daily-loss and 10% drawdown pauses.
- Universe: BIGP, DEAL, FLATA, FLATB, FLATC, GAPR, HUNT, TRND. Sessions advanced: 65 (2026-08-19 to 2026-11-18).
- Decisions at the close; fills at the next valid open; orders and fills are ledger events. No LLM, no Atlas, no network.
